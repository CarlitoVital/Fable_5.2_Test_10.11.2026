## English

> La Paz & El Alto, 1985 – 2023 🇧🇴 — 782,490 buildings, each appearing in the year
> satellites first saw a settlement on its site.
>
> By 1985 La Paz had filled its canyon. Since then, 86 % of new buildings went up on the
> altiplano of El Alto, 400 m above the city centre.
>
> ℹ️ Year = first satellite detection of a settlement on the site, not the construction
> date. Dark red = standing by 1985 · grey = undated
> ℹ️ Data: Overture Maps (Google, Microsoft, OSM) · DLR World Settlement Footprint Evolution ·
> Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal · JRC GHSL · Copernicus DEM · ESA WorldCover
> ℹ️ Tools: Python (Claude), custom 3D renderer
>
> #lapaz #elalto #bolivia #urbangrowth #urbanism #map #dataviz #gis
